Some places are worth the whole year of waiting.

We pass over the crowded top-ten lists and build each trip around a handful of destinations we'd send our own family to — from Jamaica's cliffs to the quiet backroads outside Kyoto.

Named after the places
worth cutting and keeping.

A gem isn't valuable because it's the biggest stone in the case — it's the one cut and set with care. That's the standard we hold every destination on this site to. No filler islands, no padded itineraries.

Each page here was chosen for a specific reason: a coastline, a dish, a season, a feeling you can't quite get anywhere else. We write about what to actually do once you land, not just where the airport is.
